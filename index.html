What we believe

There is no one-size-fits-all VA path

A former call-centre agent, a nursing graduate and a stay-at-home parent who loves budgeting should not all follow the same course. Your past work, your strengths and your goals point to a different path, and that is where you should start.

Honest help for your first step

The Filo Freelance Club started with a simple idea: too many Filipinos want to freelance but get lost in paid courses, scams and advice that does not fit them. We share free, practical tips, help you find the path that suits you, and keep a real person in the loop. Miggy reviews every assessment and replies personally.
